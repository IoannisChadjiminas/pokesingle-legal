1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PokeSingle ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information when you use the PokeSingle app, its scanning and collection services, and these legal and support pages.

PokeSingle is the service operator and the point of contact for decisions about personal information described here. Contact us at hello@pokesingle.com for privacy questions or requests.

This policy describes the current service. Features and analytics availability can vary by platform, app version, and service configuration. Reading this policy or accepting our Terms of Service does not itself give consent to optional processing where consent is required.

3. Camera, photos, and scanning

Camera access lets you photograph a card. The system photo picker lets you select an existing image. Images submitted for a scan are uploaded to our scanning service; recognition is not entirely on your device. On-device image preparation or text recognition may also run before upload.

When capture storage is enabled, the scanning service retains processed input images, card crops, extracted text, results, and feedback for recognition review and quality evaluation. Authorized operators may inspect this material to investigate incorrect results. Separate capture uploads may also be stored with your account where that feature is enabled. Images are not intentionally published as part of your collection.

Photograph cards only. Avoid including faces, addresses, payment details, identity documents, or other private material. You can decline camera access and use other available collection features. You can change camera and photo permissions in your device settings.

4. Analytics and advertising

When enabled for your app build, we use Mixpanel for product analytics and Google Analytics for Firebase for selected usage and outcome events. Some aggregate synchronization events are sent by our API. Analytics providers receive their own SDK or app-instance identifiers. Member activity is associated with an internal account ID after sign-in; guest activity uses SDK-generated identifiers. Signing in can associate earlier app activity with your member activity.

The current Firebase configuration denies advertising storage, advertising user-data use, and ad personalization. We do not currently use these integrations to serve personalized advertising, send email or phone lists for advertising, or request the device advertising identifier. Any future advertising use requiring additional disclosures or permission must be addressed before it starts.

The current app has no dedicated analytics on/off switch or analytics consent dialog. Notification and camera permissions do not control analytics. To object to analytics or request access or deletion, contact hello@pokesingle.com. We will explain the available controls and assess your request under applicable law. A local SDK reset or signing out does not erase historical records already held by an analytics provider.

5. Who receives information

We share information only as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Infrastructure providers: hosting, database, network, and storage services process account, collection, scan, and operational data. Cloudflare R2 is used for separate capture storage when that feature is configured.
  • Authentication providers: Apple and Google process sign-in requests when you choose their services. Their own privacy notices also apply to their independent processing.
  • Analytics providers: Mixpanel and Google receive the analytics information described above when enabled.
  • Messaging and email providers: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service handle push delivery. Resend handles sign-in and support email delivery; our support mailbox provider processes correspondence.
  • Card and price services: catalogue, image, and pricing sources supply card information. Loading remote images, opening marketplace pages, or using embedded marketplace pages can disclose your IP address, browser/device details, and the requested card or page to that service.
  • Legal and safety recipients: we may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect people, investigate abuse, enforce valid rights, or respond to lawful requests.
  • Business succession: information may transfer as part of a merger or transfer of the service, subject to applicable safeguards and notice requirements.

Service providers acting for us must handle personal information for authorized purposes under applicable data-protection obligations and protections consistent with this policy. Independent services, such as a marketplace you visit, also have their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR or comparable law applies, the relevant basis depends on the activity:

  • Providing the service: account access, requested scans, collection storage and synchronization, and requested support are processed as necessary to provide the service under our agreement with you.
  • Legitimate interests: proportionate service security, abuse prevention, fault investigation, and service improvement may rely on our interests in operating a safe and reliable service, subject to your rights and a balancing of those interests. Product analytics or recognition evaluation can rely on this basis only where legally permitted.
  • Consent: where applicable law requires consent for optional analytics, device access, notifications, or another activity, that consent is required. An operating-system permission does not replace a separate legal consent requirement.
  • Legal obligations: we may retain or disclose limited information to comply with binding legal duties.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you solely through automated processing. Automated card identification and price estimates are informational tools.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information for the purposes described here, using the type of information, feature requirements, security needs, unresolved requests, and applicable legal obligations to determine the period:

  • Account and collection records: retained while the account or guest workspace is in use, unless you remove individual content or delete the account. Local copies can remain on devices and in backups you control.
  • Scan history and captures: retained to support history, feedback, error investigation, and recognition evaluation. The current scanning service does not apply a fixed automatic expiry to every stored capture. Contact us for deletion of scan history or review images that remain outside the member-account deletion process.
  • Sessions and verification: credentials have configured validity periods and can be revoked. Expiry of a credential does not necessarily delete every related security record immediately.
  • Support and operational records: retained as needed to resolve your request, prevent repeated abuse, investigate incidents, and meet legal obligations. Duration depends on the request or incident and whether a dispute remains open.
  • Analytics: provider-side records follow the retention settings of the relevant analytics project. Account deletion does not automatically erase historical provider records. Requests for identifiable analytics data are handled separately with the provider where applicable; genuinely anonymous aggregate statistics may remain.

In-app account deletion removes account-owned database records and revokes sessions. Cleanup of separately stored account captures and external registrations is queued and may complete after database deletion. It does not automatically erase every scan-service review copy or historical analytics record. If you want those records included, contact us so we can locate and address them, subject to identity verification and applicable law. We may need the approximate scan date or another available scan reference; do not send authentication credentials.

Deletion can be subject to limited retention required by law, security needs, or an active legal claim. Backup copies may remain until the relevant backup cycle ends, with access limited to recovery and required purposes. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete a server account.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a member account: open Profile → Account & security → Delete account. Confirm the request and reauthenticate if asked.
  • Delete a guest collection: open Profile → Collection data → Delete guest collection when using a connected guest account.
  • Export your collection: open Profile → Collection data → Export collection. This exports collection information; contact us for a broader personal-data access request.
  • Manage notifications: use the notification settings in Profile to change enabled categories. You can also revoke permission in your device settings.
  • Manage camera and photos: change access in your device settings. Declining permission prevents the feature that needs that access.
  • Make a privacy request: email hello@pokesingle.com or use Profile → Help & support. Tell us whether you want access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, to object, or to withdraw a consent.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, receive a portable copy of, or restrict the use of your information, and to object to certain processing. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity. We respond within the period required by applicable law; GDPR requests are generally answered within one month, with any permitted extension explained to you. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including in the EU/EEA country where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred. You do not have to contact us first.

Guest data may be difficult to locate after you lose the device or session. Signing out, disabling notifications, or uninstalling does not replace an account-deletion request. Keep exported files private and delete copies you no longer need.

9. International processing and security

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including countries outside the European Economic Area. An EU analytics endpoint does not mean every provider or support operation is confined to the EU. Where required for a transfer, applicable safeguards must be in place, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual clauses with supplementary measures as appropriate. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

We use safeguards such as encrypted network connections, access restrictions, account-based authorization, protected credential storage, and controlled service credentials. No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and sign-in methods and let us know if you suspect unauthorized access.

10. Children

PokeSingle is a general-audience collector service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where a higher local age or parental authorization is required, that requirement also applies. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the necessary authorization, contact us so we can investigate and address it.
